# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors learning about Allen Su's research, engineering work, and projects. The site gives collaborators and other interested readers a direct way to explore that work.

## Product Purpose

A personal website with a short introduction and dedicated project write-ups. Project links should lead to content hosted on allensu.xyz where possible.

## Brand Personality

Direct, personal, understated. Preserve the author's wording when migrating existing work.

## Anti-references

Do not reproduce the old Wix portfolio shell, promotional banners, or visual theme. Avoid decorative cards, large marketing treatments, and unnecessary animation.

## Design Principles

- Match the existing site's dark theme and centered reading column.
- Keep the work and original project media prominent.
- Preserve source content and the relationships between explanations and media.
- Keep navigation simple and consistent with Home, About, and Writings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives, keyboard-accessible links and media controls, and layouts that fit narrow screens. Do not autoplay project videos.
